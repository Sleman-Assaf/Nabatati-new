ABOUT US

Nabatati – Where Technology Meets Nature

Welcome to Nabatati, an AI-powered platform designed to help farmers and plant enthusiasts detect plant diseases with ease. By simply uploading an image of a plant leaf, our intelligent system analyzes it and determines whether the plant is affected by a disease or not. This allows you to take the necessary steps to keep your plants healthy.
